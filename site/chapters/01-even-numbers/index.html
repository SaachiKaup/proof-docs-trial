    Even numbers¶

    This short chapter demonstrates the reading interaction intended for longer proof documents.

    Definition 1.1: Even integers

    An integer nnn is even when there is an integer kkk such that n=2kn = 2kn=2k.

    The integer kkk is a witness to the claim that nnn is even.

    Lemma 1.2: Squaring preserves evenness

    If nnn is even, then n2n^2n2 is even.

    Proof. By Definition 1.1, write n=2kn = 2kn=2k for an integer kkk. Hence

    n2=(2k)2=4k2=2(2k2). n^2 = (2k)^2 = 4k^2 = 2(2k^2). n2=(2k)2=4k2=2(2k2).

    Since 2k22k^22k2 is an integer, n2n^2n2 is even. □\square□

    Theorem 1.3: A sum of even squares is even

    Let aaa and bbb be even integers. Then a2+b2a^2 + b^2a2+b2 is even.

    Proof. The hypothesis means that both aaa and bbb satisfy . By , both a2a^2a2 and b2b^2b2 are even. Thus there are integers r,sr,sr,s for which a2=2ra^2 = 2ra2=2r and b2=2sb^2 = 2sb2=2s. Therefore

    a2+b2=2r+2s=2(r+s), a^2 + b^2 = 2r + 2s = 2(r+s), a2+b2=2r+2s=2(r+s),

    and r+sr+sr+s is an integer. So a2+b2a^2 + b^2a2+b2 is even. □\square□

    The underlying algebra is the distributive law, stated in OpenStax's elementary algebra text.

    The proof uses only elementary integer algebra.1

    1. OpenStax, Elementary Algebra 2e, "The Commutative and Associative Properties". ↩
