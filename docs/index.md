# Proof notes

This is a small proof-of-concept for mathematical notes that preserve a reader's place. Open the chapter from the left menu, then click an earlier definition or lemma inside the theorem.

Internal references open beside the proof. External sources remain ordinary links.

## What to try

1. Open **Theorem 1.3**.
2. Click **Definition 1.1** and **Lemma 1.2**.
3. Notice that both references remain open, and neither click moves the page.
